# Assumptions

- No real organisation identity or member records were supplied. Development content uses an explicitly fictitious union and fake names only.
- A workflow not implemented and tested is shown as unavailable, never simulated as a successful financial transaction.
- Spiced paprika (#c31b1b) is the red for negative values and destructive actions, because it retains the professional palette while standing out.
- Initial role grants are conservative: ADMIN has system and user administration but no `APPROVE`; only PRESIDENT, VICE_PRESIDENT, and TREASURER receive `APPROVE`. `PERIOD_UNLOCK` is seeded as a permission without a default grant because opening a closed accounting period needs an explicit governance decision.
- ADMIN may read investment cycles for oversight. Creating, maturing, and approving investment transactions still requires the financial roles' separate permissions.
- The configured bootstrap email has all known permissions only while the local `dev` profile is active. This allows the owner to inspect and exercise every feature during development; it does not bypass distinct-maker approval or change production role grants.
- Deactivating a member disables any linked portal account and revokes its sessions. Reactivating the member record leaves that account disabled until a separate account review. A linked active administrator or president role must be removed before member deactivation.
- Investment drafts currently use today's Accra business date for purchase. Historical investment posting waits for the period unlock and snapshot recomputation workflow, and a draft whose purchase date has passed must be recreated. Open POSTED participations remain committed until an approved maturity or settlement changes their status.
- Draft allocation ties are resolved in member-number order, then member UUID order. The cost may differ from the participation pool; the preview displays the difference rather than silently adjusting member contributions.
- A rollover invests each member's source participation plus their actual posted income, or their source participation alone for principal-only. This uses the member's closing balance rather than raw proceeds when investment cost differs from the participation pool, and it never exceeds live uninvested funds. Rollover changes the source's lifecycle status when the new draft is approved; the existing income posting remains the only member-ledger movement. Approved settlement leaves all member funds uninvested.
- The live dashboard calculates balance forward and year-to-date directly from the append-only ledger by effective date. Approved fiscal snapshots remain available as close records; dashboard snapshot reconciliation and the full Sheet B fixture are separate acceptance work.
- The organisation rollover default guides new investment drafts and is editable by `SETTINGS_WRITE`; maturity decisions still require an explicit rollover draft or approved settlement. `NO_ROLLOVER` never silently settles an investment.
- The Sheet B aggregate fixture constructs two fictitious ledger histories to reconcile the totals and percentage examples provided in the master prompt. The source workbook's complete per-member rows were not supplied, so full row-by-row workbook acceptance remains open. Percentages display two decimals (the supplied 16%/12% examples become 15.99%/12.01%).

## Investment correction revisions
- Approved reversal requires CYCLE_REPOST plus INVESTMENT_WRITE and a different APPROVE user. It is a future-only correction effective on the approval-request business date; stale requests must be cancelled and resubmitted. Original statements and closed periods remain unchanged.
- The prior approved investment and allocation revision is archived immutably, including its dates, creator, proceeds and allocations. Maturity records retain their revision. The working investment returns to DRAFT with today's purchase date, the explicitly selected future maturity date, unchanged cost/expected value, and the correction maker as its editor. Reposting requires another approval and fresh funds checks.
- Exact positive compensation is required to reverse a loss. The database now enforces exact inverse amounts, matching member/component/investment/payment references and one compensation per original entry.
- Reverse dependent cycles first and discard their returned drafts before reversing a rollover source. A source with an active descendant, or whose income is committed to another posted cycle, cannot be reversed. Historical date corrections and snapshot recomputation remain pending.
